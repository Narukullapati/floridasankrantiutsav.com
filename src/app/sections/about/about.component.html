ABOUT THE FESTIVAL

Welcome to Our Celebration

Makar Sankranti is one of India's most beloved harvest festivals, a joyful occasion that marks the sun's northward journey and the beginning of longer, warmer days. It is a time for gratitude, togetherness, and the vibrant spirit of community.

Florida Sankranti Utsav brings together the Indian American community across Florida to celebrate this cherished tradition with cultural performances, delicious food, kite flying, and the warmth of shared heritage.

All are welcome come celebrate with us!
